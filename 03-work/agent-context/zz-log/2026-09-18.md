## Multi-account CLIProxy preflight classification

- Observed a provider preflight collapse a genuinely quota-exhausted primary credential and a temporarily overloaded fallback credential into one provider-wide `quota-unavailable` state. The fallback still had capacity, but the launcher offered the all-quota reset menu because `classify_provider()` concatenated sibling runtime errors before classification.
- Changed OAuth health aggregation to classify enabled credentials separately. Any ready credential makes the provider ready; quota plus transient becomes `transient-unavailable`; quota plus an unrecognized alternate fails closed as `unknown`; `quota-unavailable` now means every enabled credential is quota-blocked. Mixed-state reasons use counts only and do not expose account labels, filenames, email addresses, or auth indexes.
- Made `cliproxy-reset-quota` selective: it posts only auth indexes currently carrying a genuine quota-exhaustion error and leaves ready, transient, unknown, and disabled siblings untouched. A provider with no quota-marked credential now refuses without a POST. The launcher remains human-confirmed and non-TTY fail-closed, with no automatic retry or inference probe.
- Corrected retry-deadline rendering to convert offset timestamps to real UTC before appending `Z` and to include the date, avoiding a locally offset time mislabeled as UTC.
- Verification passed: 56 preflight-checker tests, 52 CLIProxy launcher tests, and 35 launcher model-lock tests, including ready-plus-quota, quota-plus-transient, all-quota, unknown fallback, selective reset, no-reset, redaction, and terminal behavior. Bash/Python syntax, frontmatter, and scoped whitespace checks also passed. No live proxy cooldown was reset and no billable model request was sent.
